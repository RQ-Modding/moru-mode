# Changelog

Each section is a version of the mod, the `version` in `mod.json`. The release workflow copies the
section of a tagged version into the release notes.

## 1.0.0

First version.

- **Moru Mode**, on or off for each save, apart from TADA mode: on its page in Config's Mods tab, and
  as a row at Alice's support menu, under Change difficulty.
- **Talent growth:** Talent and Talent R equipment rolls its growth 2 to 100 times after a fight
  instead of once.
- Three of the game's conditions on that growth, each turned off on its own: the reserve's gear (the
  first army and those switched out during the quest), the fallen's, and the first world's level
  limit.
